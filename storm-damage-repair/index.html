Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Des Moines

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Shingles that are gone, cracked or curled up when you look from the yard
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward

Why Professional Storm Damage Repair Matters

Storm damage creates complex, connected repair needs that take experienced restoration professionals to handle properly. A roof leak that looks minor from inside may have done extensive hidden damage to insulation, roof decking and wall cavities, and that leads to mold growth and structural deterioration if it is not found and dried. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging and moisture meters to trace water paths and identify every affected area.

Tarping and boarding up only work when they are done with the right technique. Tarps put on the wrong way may funnel water into the building or tear loose in the wind. Experienced in storm mitigation, our team picks appropriate methods and materials to keep the building protected until permanent repair work can be scheduled.

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Check window and door seals each year and replace any caulking that has cracked
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
