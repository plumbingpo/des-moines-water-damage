Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. For emergency water extraction in Des Moines, Des Moines Restoration Co brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Professional extraction also addresses moisture that you cannot see or reach with consumer tools. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Surface extraction by itself leaves much of this behind, so our team uses specialty techniques and tools aimed at the hidden moisture. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Sprinkler discharge that has left water on floors and contents
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Add our number, (630) 274-8402, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
