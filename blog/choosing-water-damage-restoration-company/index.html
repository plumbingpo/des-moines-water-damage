Choosing a Water Damage Restoration Company in Des Moines, IA

Home / Blog / Selecting a Restoration Professional in Des Moines, IA

Picking a restoration company is a major decision for any Des Moines, IA owner dealing with water damage. The company you choose will determine how quickly your property is restored, how thoroughly the damage is addressed, how much you'll pay out of pocket, and whether you'll face secondary problems like mold growth months later. Not every outfit is qualified, and hiring the wrong one can mean inflated invoices, a job left incomplete, or something worse. Here is what to look for when sorting the best restoration firms from everyone else.

Confirm the Crew Is IICRC Certified

In the restoration industry, the leading credential comes from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC). To earn certification, a technician trains rigorously in restoration science, covering mold prevention, moisture measurement, structural drying, and proper extraction technique. Look for two current IICRC certifications on the crew: WRT (Water Damage Restoration) and ASD (Applied Structural Drying). If a company cannot prove those credentials, move on.

Make Sure They Are Insured and Licensed

Workers' compensation and general liability coverage are must-haves for a restoration contractor on your property. Request certificates of insurance and verify they're current. Without proper coverage, you could be liable for injuries or additional property damage that occur during the restoration. Licensing matters too, so confirm the firm holds whatever business licenses Des Moines or Iowa requires.

Check Availability and Arrival Speed

A water loss gets worse the longer it waits. Each hour lost adds to both the damage and the bill. Look for a company that truly answers emergencies 24/7 and can get to your area quickly. Ask specifically about their response time to Des Moines and confirm that their emergency line is answered by a live person, not a voicemail or answering service that adds delay.

Find Out What Equipment and Methods They Use

Good restoration work depends on professional-grade equipment. Three questions cover it: do they use thermal imaging to detect moisture, are their dehumidifiers LGR units (the industry standard), and is extraction done with truck-mounted equipment, which outperforms portable-only operations? Also ask about their drying monitoring process: reputable companies monitor moisture levels daily and adjust equipment based on readings, not just place equipment and hope for the best.

Look Into Reviews and Past Customers

A company's history shows up clearly in its online reviews. Search Yelp, Google, and the BBB to see if customer satisfaction, professional conduct, and work quality hold up consistently. The most useful reviews talk about communication, punctuality, help with insurance, and how the finished restoration turned out. Don't hesitate to ask for references from recent water damage projects in Des Moines.

Understand Pricing and Insurance Billing

Expect a detailed estimate up front, along with a plain explanation of the pricing, from any firm worth hiring. Good firms often price jobs with industry-standard software such as Xactimate, which insurers know and accept. Ask whether the company works directly with insurance carriers and whether they can bill your insurance directly. A demand for big upfront money, or a refusal to deal with your insurer, is a warning sign.
