Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in Des Moines or the surrounding area, Des Moines Restoration Co runs an emergency response around the clock. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room

Why Professional Burst Pipe Repair Matters

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.
